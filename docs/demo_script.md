## Timed 2-Minute Demo Path (0:00 – 2:00)

### Step 1: Regulator Map & The 1 Nov 2026 Mandate (0:00 – 0:20)
* **Who Clicks (Tab 1 — /regulator):** Hover over a green kiln (K001), a red kiln (K003), and a shaded fire-density cell on the Leaflet map.
* **Who Speaks:**
  > "Tomorrow—1 November 2026—CAQM Statutory Direction No. 92 steps up the mandatory paddy-straw co-firing target for brick kilns in Punjab and non-NCR Haryana from 20% to 30%. Here on our Regulator Dashboard (using cached NASA FIRMS satellite fire detections as a 0-to-1 relative supply index and labelled sample kiln data), officers see live which kilns meet the 30% target in green and which are below target in red."

### Step 2: Kiln Calculator — Straw Needed & Compliance Gap (0:20 – 0:40)
* **Who Clicks (Switch to Tab 2 — /kiln):** Select K003 (Sample Kiln 3, Malerkotla) with 120 tonnes monthly fuel consumption. Highlight the **36 tonnes needed**, **10 tonnes delivered**, and **26 tonnes gap**.
* **Who Speaks:**
  > "Switching to the Kiln Owner view for Sample Kiln 3: for 120 tonnes of monthly fuel in November 2026, our rule-based calculator shows a 30% requirement of 36 tonnes of straw. They have 10 tonnes delivered so far, leaving a 26-tonne shortfall."

### Step 3: Ranked Suppliers & Delivered Cost vs. Coal (0:40 – 1:00)
* **Who Clicks (Stay on Tab 2 — /kiln):** Scroll slightly to the **Ranked Nearby Suppliers** table for K003. Point to S005 (Sample Aggregator, 12.4 km away, chopped straw) showing **₹2,025/tonne delivered** vs. the **₹9,000/tonne coal** placeholder.
* **Who Speaks:**
  > "Traditional hand-fed kilns tell us they prefer chopped straw over pellets. Below the calculator, Parali Mitra ranks nearby farmers, aggregators, and pellet units by true delivered cost—including distance and chopping assumptions—showing chopped straw from Sample Aggregator 12.4 km away at ₹2,025 per tonne delivered versus ₹9,000 for coal."

### Step 4: Supplier App (Phone View) — Listing Straw & Finding Kilns (1:00 – 1:25)
* **Who Clicks (Switch to Tab 3 — /supplier in mobile view):**
  1. Toggle language briefly (English / हिन्दी) to show bilingual support from i18n.
  2. Fill/show Supplier Form: Sample Aggregator, Village Malerkotla, Form chopped, Tonnes 40, Price 1800 -> click **Find Nearest Kilns**.
  3. On Screen 3 (Nearest Kilns), click **Deliver Here** next to K003 - Sample Kiln 3.
* **Who Speaks:**
  > "Now on the mobile Supplier App—available in English and Hindi—a farmer or aggregator in Malerkotla lists chopped straw and immediately sees the nearest kilns ranked by distance and delivered price. They select Sample Kiln 3."

### Step 5: Confirm Delivery & Issue Evidence Receipt (1:25 – 1:45)
* **Who Clicks (Stay on Tab 3 — /supplier):** Enter 26 tonnes delivered, photo reference slip_011.jpg, and click **Confirm Delivery & Get Receipt**. Hold for 3 seconds on the **Receipt Screen** (Receipt R011, "SAMPLE DATA" badge visible).
* **Who Speaks:**
  > "They log a 26-tonne delivery with a weighbridge slip photo reference and receive a timestamped receipt. This receipt is a verifiable record that straw left the field—stronger evidence than a selfie, supporting regulatory audits."

### Step 6: Live Compliance Ledger Update (1:45 – 2:00)
* **Who Clicks (Switch back to Tab 1 — /regulator):** Do not refresh the browser—let the 5-second poll automatically flip K003 (Sample Kiln 3) from **Red (8.3%)** to **Green (30.0% — meets_target)** as the KPI card and bar chart jump up.
* **Who Speaks:**
  > "Back on the Regulator Dashboard—without even refreshing—Sample Kiln 3 updates within 5 seconds from 8.3% to 30%, turning green on the map and moving the district's live compliance bar to target."
